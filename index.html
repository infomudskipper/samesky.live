Under the Same SKY

“Two children. Two countries. One planet — told through the voices of children.”

Scroll

An immersive exhibition across India and Germany, revealing a universal truth — beyond all borders, we share one sky, one planet, one future. Running 4 to 6 weeks at cultural institutions in both countries.

The Experience
Three ways to live it

Visitors don’t just see the exhibition — they feel it, live inside it, and leave changed by it.

01
Feel

Walk through a gallery of paired photographs — one from India, one from Germany. No labels. You guess. You realise the lines between countries begin to blur.

02
Immerse

At the heart of the exhibition — a cinematic short film shot across Germany and India. Two children. Two worlds. Their words mirror each other exactly.

03
Connect

A living wall installation where children leave letters, drawings, and reflections. Notes written in India appear in Germany — and vice versa. A live exchange across borders.
